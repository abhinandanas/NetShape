Why Leaky Bucket Works: CBR Output

No matter how volatile the arrival bursts from 4K IPL or Steam downloads are, the leaky bucket converts variable bit rate (VBR) bursts into a constant bit rate (CBR) output, avoiding packet collisions at the router bottleneck.

Bypass vs Shaped Comparison: Failure Mode

In Bypass mode (Shaper OFF), when aggregate traffic spikes over 5.0 pkt/s, the ISP link drops packets indiscriminately, destroying Zoom's UDP stream. With Leaky Bucket, bursts are buffered smoothly.
